Ayurveda at Araliyā

At Araliyā Ayurveda, we offer a wide range of authentic Ayurvedic treatments and wellness packages designed to restore balance, rejuvenate the body, and enhance overall wellbeing. Our therapies include personalized doctor consultations, traditional full-body and specialized massages, herbal treatments like Shirodhara, Pottali Sweda, and targeted therapies for back, neck, shoulder, foot, and facial care.

We also provide curated wellness packages for weight management, stress relief, detoxification, skin rejuvenation, muscle recovery, sleep improvement, sinus relief, and women’s health. Each treatment and package is tailored to harmonize the body, mind, and spirit, blending ancient Ayurvedic wisdom with modern care for lasting wellness.
